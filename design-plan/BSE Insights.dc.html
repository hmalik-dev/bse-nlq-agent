How many tickets did we sell for Nets home games last month?

We sold 26,412 tickets in August 2026 — all of them for upcoming games, since the Nets play no home games in August.

READ-ONLY
1-- Tickets sold in Aug 2026 for Nets home fixtures
2SELECT e.name AS event,
3 e.starts_at AS date,
4 COUNT(t.id) AS tickets_sold,
5 ROUND(AVG(t.price), 2) AS avg_price,
6 SUM(t.price) AS gate_revenue
7FROM tickets t
8JOIN orders o ON o.id = t.order_id
9JOIN events e ON e.id = t.event_id
10JOIN teams h ON h.id = e.home_team_id
11WHERE h.slug = 'nets'
12 AND o.purchased_at >= '2026-08-01'
13 AND o.purchased_at < '2026-09-01'
14 AND t.status = 'sold'
15GROUP BY e.id, e.name, e.starts_at
16ORDER BY tickets_sold DESC
17LIMIT 8;
